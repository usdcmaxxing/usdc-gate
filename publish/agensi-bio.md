# 🧬 Agensi Creator Bio

*Display name suggestion:* **USDC Gate** · *handle:* `usdc-gate` or `usdcmaxxing`

The bio is one or two lines, shown right under the follower count. The best ones on the
platform lead with **credibility** or **the buyer's benefit**, not "AI skill creator".

---

## Recommended (pick one)

### 🥇 Option A — credibility-first
> We run 16 paid x402 endpoints in production. These are the skills that made them work — and the mistakes that cost us time.

*Why it works:* instant proof. Not "I know x402" — "I ship x402, daily."

### 🥈 Option B — benefit-first
> Practical x402 and agent-payment skills, distilled from production systems. No theory — what actually shipped and what broke.

*Why it works:* tells the buyer exactly what they get, and signals the content is battle-tested.

### 🥉 Option C — short + specific
> Build paid APIs that AI agents pay for, per call, in USDC. Skills from a live 16-endpoint deployment.

*Why it works:* names the job (paid APIs for agents) and the proof (16 endpoints).

## What NOT to write

- ❌ "AI skill creator" — every second profile says this (Roy Yuen does). It's noise.
- ❌ "Passionate about AI and helping people" — no information.
- ❌ Anything longer than ~160 characters — it gets truncated on cards.

---

## The pattern the top creators use

| Creator | Bio | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Samuel Rose | Founder of Agensi | authority |
| Ignasi | AI developer building practical skills for Claude Code & agents: cut token costs, ship faster with git | role + concrete benefit |
| AI Agent Store | We build AI agent skill packages for content creators. Specializing in Chinese social media automation | niche + specialization |

**Formula:** `who you are` + `what you make` + `the concrete benefit or niche`.

Our version: *who* = operators of a live x402 service, *what* = skills, *benefit* = skip the mistakes.
