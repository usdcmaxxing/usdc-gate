# LinkedIn Post

**Headline:** I built a paid data marketplace for AI agents — here's what I learned

**Body:**

AI agents are transacting on-chain at massive scale. x402 processed 196 million transactions in 2026, settling $42 million in USDC.

But there's a gap: agents need reliable data — sanctions lists, risk scores, price history — and almost nobody sells it in a way they can buy programmatically.

So I built USDC Gate: a paid data marketplace where agents pay per call in USDC via x402.

No account. No API key. No subscription. Just pay and get data.

What's for sale:
• Sanctions & risk dataset (623 records, $0.02)
• Memecoin shadow book (2,969 picks, $0.02)
• Token contract registry (500 tokens, $0.02)
• x402 service registry (4,000 services, $0.05)
• Plus 8 more datasets and 3 APIs

The interesting part: I didn't build new data pipelines. I packaged data I already had.

The honest economics: agent-payment volume is thin. This isn't a primary income stream — it's an acquisition funnel. The real revenue is selling the same backend to human compliance teams.

Open source: https://github.com/usdcmaxxing/usdc-gate
